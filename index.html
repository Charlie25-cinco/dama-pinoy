      Quick reference

      How to Play

      1. Red moves first in every game in Dama Pinoy. Choose your Game 1 color (or Player 1's color in Two Players). The board is shown from Player 1's side, so their pieces stay at the bottom even when they play Blue; the replay keeps that game's view. In Best of 3, 5, or 9, the players exchange colors each game; names and scores stay with their players. First to 2, 3, or 5 wins respectively takes the match. Drawn games count toward the limit without scoring; at the limit, the higher series score wins or a tie draws the match.
      2. Pieces move diagonally forward. A normal piece steps one dark square forward.
      3. Captures are mandatory. Jump over an adjacent enemy piece to the empty square beyond — forward or backward.
      4. Choose the biggest capture. You must follow the capture path that removes the most pieces.
      5. Reach the opposite end to become a Dama. If the capture chain continues after reaching the last row, keep jumping as an ordinary piece. It becomes a Dama only when the turn ends there.
      6. A Dama moves any distance along an empty diagonal. It captures by jumping an adjacent enemy piece and landing on the very next square beyond it, forward or backward.
      7. Capture all opponent pieces or block all their moves to win. If both sides make 100 moves with no capture or promotion, the game is a draw.

      Playing vs Computer? Choose Red to move first in Game 1, or Blue to let the computer open as Red. In a series match, you and the computer swap colors after each game. The computer follows the same Dama rules and keeps your chosen Easy, Medium, or Hard difficulty for the whole match.

      Statistics: Completed games and matches are saved on this device. Local two-player results are recorded from Player 1's perspective; vs Computer results are from your perspective. Reset Statistics clears only the statistics record.

      Replay files: Export a completed replay from Game Over or the replay viewer. Import a Dama Pinoy replay JSON file from the menu or viewer to watch it without replacing the saved Last Replay.

      Online Multiplayer: Two guests can play a six-character room code. The host chooses a Game 1 color and a Single Game or Best of 3, 5, or 9 series; the guest joins those settings. Red opens every game, players swap colors each game, and drawn games count toward the limit. Both players confirm each next game or a new series. An internet connection is required; local play still works offline.
