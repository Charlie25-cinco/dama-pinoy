Dama Pinoy

Traditional Filipino Checkers

Two players, one board. Mandatory captures, longest-chain jumps, and Dama promotions — played the way it is at home.

  • Local 2-player
  • Works offline
  • No install

Quick reference

How to Play

  1. Red moves first. Players take turns on the same device.
  2. Pieces move diagonally forward. A normal piece steps one dark square forward.
  3. Captures are mandatory. Jump over an adjacent enemy piece to the empty square beyond — forward or backward.
  4. Choose the biggest capture. You must follow the capture path that removes the most pieces.
  5. Reach the opposite end to become a Dama. Promotion happens immediately and ends that turn.
  6. A Dama moves and captures in all diagonal directions, sliding any distance over empty squares.
  7. Capture all opponent pieces or block all their moves to win. If both sides make 100 moves with no capture or promotion, the game is a draw.
