# Dama Pinoy

Traditional Filipino Dama checkers for local two-player, computer, or online room-code play. Local and online games support Single Game and Best of 3, 5, or 9. Review the latest local replay and track local statistics. The local game can be installed and played offline after its first successful load; online play requires internet access.

## Run locally

This is a static, dependency-free HTML/CSS/JavaScript app. Serve the project directory using a local web server, then open `index.html` in a modern browser. For the Laragon layout used by this project (`C:\laragon\www\dama-pinoy`), open `http://localhost/dama-pinoy/` while Laragon is running. A direct `file://` opening works for the game, but service workers and offline installation require localhost or HTTPS.

Local play needs no package installation, account, backend, or external assets. Online Multiplayer needs a configured Supabase project; its JavaScript client loads only when online play is opened. Service-worker installation and offline support depend on browser support.

## Play

1. Select **Two Players** or **vs Computer** and choose **Single Game**, **Best of 3**, **Best of 5**, or **Best of 9**. Choose Player 1's color (or your color against the computer) for Game 1. Red is the default; for the computer, select Easy, Medium, or Hard.
2. Select **Start Game** or **Start Match**. **Red always moves first in Dama Pinoy**. The board faces Player 1 (you in vs Computer), so your pieces appear at the bottom even when you choose Blue. In a series, the players swap colors each game, including after a draw, and the board view changes with them. If you choose Blue against the computer in Game 1, the computer opens as Red; you open as Red in Game 2. The chosen computer difficulty stays fixed for that match. Captures are mandatory and must remove the most pieces; a piece that reaches the far row mid-chain continues as an ordinary piece and promotes only if that turn ends there.
3. The first to 2, 3, or 5 wins takes a Best of 3, 5, or 9 match. A drawn game gives neither side a point but counts toward the 3-, 5-, or 9-game limit. At that limit, the higher series score wins even if neither side reached the win target; a tied series is a drawn match. Names, series points, and all-time wins follow the players across color swaps. Use **Next Game** to continue or **New Match** to reset the series after it ends.
4. Select **View Replay** after a game or **Last Replay** from the menu. The replay uses the view saved for that game. Step with Left/Right, play or pause with Space, and exit with Escape. The most recent completed replay is saved on this device. Use **Export Replay** on Game Over or in the viewer to download its JSON file. Use **Import Replay** on the menu or in the viewer to watch a previously exported file; importing does not replace Last Replay or change the active game or statistics. These controls also work offline and when opening `index.html` directly.
5. Select **Statistics** from the title screen or the game screen to review completed games. Statistics are recorded only when a game finishes. Local two-player results are recorded from Player 1's perspective; vs Computer results are from your perspective. Drawn series count as played but not as series wins or losses. **Reset Statistics** requires confirmation and keeps names, preferences, the saved replay, the active game, and all-time win counters.
6. Select **Online Multiplayer** and enter a guest name. A host chooses their Game 1 color and **Single Game**, **Best of 3**, **Best of 5**, or **Best of 9**, then shares the six-character room code. The guest inherits those fixed room options. Red opens every game, players swap colors between series games, and both players confirm **Next Game**, **Rematch**, or **New Series** as appropriate. Draws consume a game without awarding a point. Online games do not change local Statistics, all-time scores, or Last Replay.
7. Use **Join as Spectator** with an active room code to watch without occupying a player seat. Up to five spectators may watch; their board faces Red and has no move or rematch controls. Players see the spectator names. Leaving as a spectator does not close the room. Disconnected spectator slots expire after 60 seconds.
8. Completed online games are recorded on the server. Use **View Replay** or **Export Replay** after a game, or **Load Recent Replays** in the online menu for your latest 20 games. Viewing and export require internet; an exported JSON file can be imported offline. Online replay viewing never replaces local Last Replay. Player access follows the original anonymous Auth session; clearing browser data loses that identity. Spectators can read a room's replays only while their membership is current and the room is not closed.
9. When reopening Online Multiplayer with a saved room, choose **Rejoin Room** to fetch its latest board, history, colors, scores, and result, or **Leave Room** to discard the saved room. The existing 60-second player disconnect grace still applies; a saved room does not reserve a seat beyond that grace.

Scores, statistics, preferences, and the most recent replay are stored locally in the browser. Previous color-keyed names and win counts are carried forward as Player 1 and Player 2 records; previously saved replays remain viewable. Unfinished matches and restarted games are not added to statistics and do not survive a reload. **How to Play** in the app explains captures, Dama promotion, and draw rules.

Replay downloads use JSON with `format: "dama-pinoy-replay"` and `version: 1`, separate from the internal saved replay version. The file records `exportedAt`, player names and colors keyed by `P1`/`P2` (P1 is the human against AI), game mode, series format and game number, AI difficulty when applicable, result, Red's opening turn, the original board view, and each move's ordered captures and promotion. `game.seriesFormat` is `single`, `bestOf3`, `bestOf5`, or `bestOf9`; `game.result` is `P1`, `P2`, or `draw`; and `game.view` is `red-bottom` or `blue-bottom`. Each move identifies its `P1`/`P2` player, starting and ending coordinates, `normal`/`dama` piece, ordered captured coordinates, and promotion flag. Import accepts only this file format and validates its moves before opening the read-only viewer. An imported replay is kept in memory only; exiting it returns to the previous screen.

## Online setup and deployment

### Optional accounts (v2.3)

Select **Log In / Sign Up** for a simple email/password **Log In** form. Choose **Create Account** to enter your email, password (at least 8 characters), and display name, or **Forgot Password?** to reset using an email code. Only the actions needed for the current step are shown; **Back to Log In** returns from signup/reset, and **Back to Menu** keeps guest/local play available without an account. **Upgrade Guest / Log In** is also available inside online rooms. Account creation upgrades a guest using Supabase `updateUser`, preserving the anonymous Auth UUID, room seat, replay ownership, local statistics, Last Replay, and preferences. Confirm the signup email in the same browser that requested it. The UI no longer offers magic-link requests or passwordless signup; previously issued callback links remain supported, and existing passwordless accounts can set a password using **Forgot Password?**. Logging into a different account or logging out requires leaving any current/saved room first; accounts cannot silently take another identity's seat. **Log In Again / Confirm Identity** reauthenticates the same account without dropping its seat, including before deletion or after session expiry.

Profiles are private; display names contain 1–20 trimmed characters without control characters or `< >`. New rooms use the profile name; recorded game names stay fixed. Account **Statistics** contain only server-validated online games/matches completed while the identity has an account profile. Local/AI statistics are not uploaded or merged and reappear after logout. Historical guest replays follow a same-UUID upgrade; historical guest statistics are not backfilled. Online replays and account statistics follow login across devices. A new guest identity cannot access a previous account's records.

**Delete Account** requires typing `DELETE` and login within the past 15 minutes. It deletes Auth identity, profile, and private statistics, closes player rooms, removes spectator membership, and replaces identity/name references in shared room/move/result/replay records with a random tombstone and “Deleted player”. Opponents retain their shared replays; local data is unchanged. Backups and mail-delivery logs follow the platform operator's retention policy. Operator deletion of a room/game also deletes its associated online replays/statistics.

#### Supabase Auth configuration

1. Apply `supabase/migrations/20261027_player_accounts.sql` after previous migrations, then redeploy `online-room` with JWT verification enabled. Guest seat/replay IDs already are verified Auth UUIDs: no duplicate identity columns or nullable seat ownership are introduced.
2. In **Authentication → Providers → Email**, enable email/password signups and email confirmation. Keep anonymous sign-in enabled and require passwords of at least 8 characters. Supabase stores passwords; the app never stores them in its tables, files, logs, or preferences.
3. Set **Authentication → URL Configuration → Site URL** to the deployed HTTPS app URL. Add the exact app callback URL (including its subdirectory/`index.html` path) to **Redirect URLs**. Add explicit localhost URLs for development only. Signup/magic links use PKCE and the current path as callback; avoid broad production wildcard redirects. Accounts do not work over `file://`. Open confirmation/magic links in the browser that initiated them. Password reset uses a typed email OTP instead.
4. Keep **Confirm signup/email change** and **Magic Link** templates using `{{ .ConfirmationURL }}`. For **Reset Password only**, show the numeric **`{{ .Token }}`**, not a login/reset link. Suggested body: `<h2>Reset your Dama Pinoy password</h2><p>Enter this code in Dama Pinoy:</p><p><strong>{{ .Token }}</strong></p><p>Use the newest code. Never share it. If you did not request a reset, ignore this email.</p>`. Supabase enforces code expiry, one-time use, and server rate limits; configure the email OTP length (6–10 digits) and expiration in Auth settings. Expired sessions still prompt for login or an explicit return to guest mode.
5. **Free first:** Supabase's built-in mail sender supports limited development testing with restrictive recipient/rate limits. Verify the project's restrictions before expecting delivery to players. A free-tier SMTP provider (such as Resend with a verified sender domain) may suit small deployments. Email/delivery metadata is processed by both providers; review regions and retention policies. **Paid option:** production SMTP plans add higher quotas, delivery monitoring, support, and domain controls. Choose a provider explicitly and put SMTP credentials only in Supabase dashboard/server settings, never in this repository.

Session restoration loads the existing Supabase client when a saved session/Auth callback exists, or when account/online UI is opened. Offline local play does not need it. Auth actions require HTTPS (localhost is allowed for development) and internet. The SDK persists its session; the service worker never caches Auth/API responses or query-bearing callback pages. Never publish emails, passwords, tokens, or callback codes in logs/screenshots.

Email callbacks are explicitly processed once with `exchangeCodeForSession` and PKCE; automatic SDK URL detection is disabled to avoid duplicate exchanges and losing the recovery marker. A verified magic link opens the account profile, a recovery link opens **Save New Password**, and an email-confirmed guest upgrade keeps its original UUID/room. Recovery UI survives a same-tab refresh while the matching session is valid (the UI marker expires after one hour). Expired/reused links, provider errors, and missing browser verifiers show visible instructions instead of silently returning to login. Implicit bearer-token callbacks are not accepted as a fallback to PKCE.

For the deployed app, configure both Site URL and the allowed redirect `https://dama-pinoy.vercel.app/`; also allow the exact `/index.html` URL if that is used to request links. Signup/magic templates must link to Supabase's **`{{ .ConfirmationURL }}`**, not just `{{ .SiteURL }}` or the app's login page. Disable email link tracking/rewriting for Auth messages where supported; email scanners or previously opened links can consume one-time links. Request one fresh link at a time and use the latest email in the browser/device that requested it, not an email app's embedded browser. Do not clear site data while waiting for a link, as that removes the PKCE verifier.

**Password reset (v2.4):** choose **Forgot Password?**, enter your email, then **Send Reset Code**. The form advances to the code step: enter the newest code and choose **Verify Reset Code**, then **Save New Password**. You can read the email on any device; no same-browser verifier is needed for typed codes. **Resend Reset Code** appears only at the code step and has a one-minute browser cooldown in addition to server limits. If you refresh before verification or start on another browser, start **Forgot Password?** there and request a new code. Codes and the reset email are not saved in browser storage or URLs. Verification happens in a non-persisted client before the verified recovery session is installed; leave any saved/current room before requesting or verifying recovery to prevent switching room identities. Existing PKCE recovery links remain readable for compatibility, but new reset emails must contain the OTP. After verification, the same-tab recovery form can survive refresh; after saving, use the new password to log in anywhere.

1. Create a Supabase project (Supabase Free is sufficient for initial testing). In Authentication, enable **Anonymous Sign-Ins**. In Realtime Settings, turn off **Allow public access** so room channels require authorization. Choose a region appropriate for your players; guest names and moves are stored in that project's region.
2. Apply [`supabase/migrations/20261024_online_single.sql`](supabase/migrations/20261024_online_single.sql), then [`supabase/migrations/20261025_online_match_options.sql`](supabase/migrations/20261025_online_match_options.sql), in the SQL editor. They create and upgrade the four room/match tables, constraints, member-only RLS reads, private Broadcast/Presence policies, and service-role-only transactional functions. The upgrade closes legacy rooms that already contain multiple v2.0 rematches so they cannot be mistaken for a partial series. Do not disable JWT verification for the Edge Function.
   For v2.2, also apply [`supabase/migrations/20261026_online_replays_spectators.sql`](supabase/migrations/20261026_online_replays_spectators.sql) before deploying the updated Edge Function. It adds RLS-protected replay/spectator tables, atomic replay recording, spectator membership RPCs, and private spectator channel policies. New replays are recorded for games completed after this migration; historical games are not backfilled. Existing room `version` is reused. With a CLI-linked project, use `supabase db push --linked`, then `supabase functions deploy online-room --project-ref YOUR_PROJECT_REF` (JWT verification stays enabled).
3. Deploy [`supabase/functions/online-room/index.ts`](supabase/functions/online-room/index.ts) as an Edge Function named `online-room` using the Supabase CLI or dashboard. The function needs `SUPABASE_URL`, `SUPABASE_ANON_KEY`, and `SUPABASE_SERVICE_ROLE_KEY` **only in its server-side environment**. Never put the service-role key in the repository or browser. Supabase normally provides these values to its Edge Functions; check the project configuration before deployment.
4. Set the project URL and **publishable** key in [`online-config.js`](online-config.js). These are public browser values. Update the service-worker cache version when changing this cached file on an installed app. Online rooms remain unavailable until both values are set; local play still works.
5. Sync the full project (including `icons/`, `manifest.json`, `online-*.js`, and `supabase/`) to the GitHub repository before connecting its `main` branch to a new Vercel static site. Vercel Hobby and Supabase Free are suitable for small-scale use; paid plans increase quotas, capacity, and support. Enable HTTPS (provided by Vercel) and test two separate browsers/devices against the live Supabase project.

Online guests use invisible Supabase anonymous authentication; optional accounts use the same verified Auth identity model. The verified Auth user ID, not a browser-supplied guest ID, owns each room seat. Realtime messages only signal clients to fetch the database state; moves are checked against the Dama rules in the Edge Function and committed atomically. A disconnected player has up to 60 seconds to reconnect; idle rooms become unusable after 30 minutes. Closed rooms and anonymous Auth users need periodic retention cleanup configured by the project operator. Online mode requires internet; the Supabase CDN, API, and WebSocket are not cached by the service worker.

Replay retention follows room retention: deleting a room or game also deletes its online replays. No automatic retention schedule is included. Choose a cleanup policy before production use and tell players how long records are retained. Anonymous identities are device/browser-specific, not a recoverable account. Supabase Free has usage quotas; paid plans increase capacity but do not replace privacy/retention configuration. For Vercel static deployment, use preset **Other**, no build command, and repository-root output; no server secrets belong in frontend environment variables.

## Development

`index.html` contains the screens, `style.css` the themes and responsive layout, `script.js` the local rules/AI/match/replay/statistics behavior, `online-rules.js` the pure online rules shared with the server, `online.js` the guest client, and `manifest.json`, `service-worker.js`, and `icons/` the PWA assets. Run `node --check script.js`, `node --check online-rules.js`, `node --check online.js`, and `node --check service-worker.js` for syntax checks. Test online rules against the local rules and exercise two authenticated browsers against a configured project, including host color and format selection, room creation/join, Red opening after color swaps, series scoring and draws, next-game/new-series confirmation, turn locking, captures, promotions, disconnect/reconnect, optimistic-move rollback, and rejection of stale moves. Recheck local modes, replay transfer, statistics, offline reload, and desktop/mobile layouts.

See [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md) for implementation conventions and [CHANGELOG.md](CHANGELOG.md) for release changes.
